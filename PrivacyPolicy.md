# 🔒 Gambo – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** April 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Gambo** (the “Bot”) handles information when you use it on Discord.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge this policy. **Terms of Service** are published separately (e.g. on your support server or bot listing).

---

### 1. Who is responsible

The **operator of Gambo** (the development / hosting team) is responsible for data the Bot stores for its features.  
**Contact for privacy questions or deletion requests:** *https://discord.gg/invite/ADrkcEfMWA.*

**Discord** runs the platform (accounts, servers, messages). Discord’s own terms and privacy policy apply to their services.

---

### 2. What we collect

We keep **only what we need** to run the Bot. This routinely includes:

- **Discord user ID** — to know whose balance, bets, achievements, and settings belong to whom.  
- **Server (guild) ID** — so economy and games are **per server** where the Bot is used.  
- **Display name** — as shown on Discord, for leaderboards and in-game labels (we may update it when you interact with the Bot).  
- **Virtual economy data** — balances, reward cooldowns, streaks, and similar game state.  
- **Game and feature activity** — e.g. bets, match state for mini-games, prediction events, virtual stock holdings, achievement progress and unlocks, as required by each command.  
- **Identifiers for Discord messages/channels** — where the Bot must edit or track a specific message (e.g. live leaderboards or open predictions).  
- **Voting rewards** — your user ID and **when** you last received a vote-based reward, so we can enforce cooldowns and optional reminders.  
- **Premium status** — linked to your Discord account (e.g. via roles and internal records) and **when** weekly perks were last claimed; we do **not** store payment card details inside the Bot.

We **do not** aim to collect sensitive categories of data (e.g. health, government IDs). **Premium payments** are handled by **Patreon** (or another processor you use); payment details stay with them.

**Temporary data:** Some information exists only in memory for a short time (e.g. rate limits) and is not kept as a long-term profile.

---

### 3. Why we use it (legal bases)

Where laws like the **GDPR** apply, we generally rely on:

- **Providing the service** you use when the Bot is on a server and you run commands.  
- **Legitimate interests** in keeping the Bot fair, secure, and abuse-resistant (e.g. duplicate vote protection, operational backups).  
- **Consent** only where the law requires it for something optional (not for core Bot operation).

*If you need a formal legal mapping for your country or company form, consult a qualified professional.*

---

### 4. How we use it

To run economy commands, games, predictions, football sims, stocks, achievements, leaderboards, vote rewards, and Premium perks; to send **vote reminders** only where that logic applies; and to report **anonymous aggregate stats** about the Bot to listing services (e.g. how many servers use the Bot), **not** a list of individual users.

---

### 5. Third-party services

| Provider | Role |
|----------|------|
| **Discord** | Platform, identity, chat, and permissions. |
| **Top.gg** | Vote tracking and rewards; may receive your **Discord user ID** when you vote so the Bot can credit you. Their policies apply. |

We **do not sell** your personal data. We do **not** use the Bot to run cross-site ad tracking.

### 6. Storage and security

Data is stored on **servers or infrastructure controlled by the operator** (e.g. a database on the machine that hosts the Bot). Access should be limited to **trusted people** maintaining Gambo. We may keep **backups** for recovery; treat those with the same care as live data.

No online service is perfectly secure; we are not responsible for failures that originate solely with Discord, hosting providers, or events outside our reasonable control (see also your TOS).

---

### 7. How long we keep data

We keep data **while the Bot is used** and **as long as needed** for the features above, unless you ask for deletion (where possible) or we wipe data for maintenance, as described in our TOS.

**To request deletion or information:** contact us through the **official support channel** above. Deleting your data may **remove virtual progress and balances** and may be irreversible.
