# Gambo — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be processed when you use Gambo (the "Bot" or "Service"), a Discord bot that may provide entertainment features, virtual currency, games, leaderboards, simulated markets, and related functionality.

The Service operates through Discord and may rely on external hosting, infrastructure, APIs, databases, and other service providers.

This Policy explains the categories of information that may be processed, the purposes of processing, potential recipients, retention considerations, and privacy rights that may apply.

The actual information processed depends on the features enabled, how you interact with the Bot, and the technical configuration of the Service.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to information processed through Gambo and its related features.

It does not govern Discord's independent processing of personal data, nor the independent processing practices of other third-party services. Those services have their own privacy policies.

This Policy should be read alongside Gambo's Terms of Service.

## 3. Categories of Information

Depending on the features used and the information made available to the Bot, Gambo may process the following categories:

### 3.1 Discord and Server Identifiers

The Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role identifiers, and other identifiers needed to respond to commands and associate activity with the correct user or server.

A Discord username, display name, or avatar may be available through Discord or an interaction. The Bot should only retain such information where needed for an enabled feature or a legitimate operational purpose.

### 3.2 Virtual Economy and Game Data

If relevant features are enabled, the Bot may process virtual balances, inventory, transaction records, game outcomes, cooldowns, achievements, rankings, simulated market activity, and related state.

This information is used to provide the requested functionality, maintain consistent records, detect abuse, and correct errors.

### 3.3 Commands and Interaction Data

The Bot may process command names, interaction metadata, timestamps, server context, and information that you intentionally submit while using a command.

The Bot should not collect or retain message content unrelated to its functions unless the relevant feature actually requires it and such processing is lawful.

### 3.4 Technical and Security Information

Depending on the hosting and software configuration, technical information may be processed to maintain service availability, diagnose errors, investigate abuse, and protect the Service.

Examples may include error reports, operational events, request timestamps, and security logs. Hosting or infrastructure providers may separately generate technical logs under their own practices.

### 3.5 Support Communications

If you contact support or submit a bug report, the information you include may be processed to respond, investigate the issue, and improve the Service.

Avoid including passwords, payment credentials, sensitive personal information, or information about other people unless strictly necessary and lawful.

### 3.6 Payment-Related Information

If Gambo offers paid features, a third-party payment provider may process payment and transaction information.

The Bot operator should not receive or store full payment-card credentials unless a specific lawful and appropriately secured payment arrangement requires it. Payment details are generally handled by the relevant payment provider.

The exact information available to the Service depends on the payment system used.

## 4. Purposes of Processing

Information may be processed, where applicable, for the following purposes:

- operating commands and requested features;
- maintaining virtual balances, inventory, transactions, and game state;
- providing leaderboards and other user-requested functionality;
- preventing fraud, cheating, exploitation, spam, and unauthorized access;
- diagnosing technical errors and maintaining security;
- responding to support requests;
- administering purchases, subscriptions, and payment-related disputes if paid features are offered;
- complying with legal obligations and responding to valid legal requests;
- protecting the rights, users, systems, and legitimate interests associated with the Service.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes that are incompatible with the purposes for which it was collected, unless the law permits the further processing.

## 5. Legal Bases Where GDPR Applies

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personal data must be processed on a valid legal basis.

Depending on the specific processing activity, a relevant legal basis may include:

- **Performance of a contract:** where processing is necessary to provide a feature requested under applicable terms.
- **Legitimate interests:** where processing is necessary for purposes such as preventing abuse, securing the Bot, or maintaining reliable records, provided those interests are not overridden by the individual's rights and interests.
- **Legal obligation:** where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement.
- **Consent:** where consent is required and has validly been obtained.

The appropriate legal basis must be assessed for each actual processing activity. This Policy does not itself create consent where valid consent has not been obtained.

Where processing relies on consent, consent may be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

## 6. Data Minimization and Accuracy

The Service should process only information reasonably necessary for its stated functions and retain it only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose or legal obligation.

Users should avoid submitting personal information that is not required by a Bot feature.

Where appropriate and technically feasible, inaccurate records may be corrected and unnecessary information removed, subject to legitimate recordkeeping needs and applicable law.

## 7. Hosting and Infrastructure

Gambo uses hosting infrastructure and may depend on third-party technical services. Sparked Host may be involved in hosting the Service.

Depending on the actual configuration, a hosting or infrastructure provider may process technical information, system logs, backups, or other data necessary to operate and secure its services.

The provider's own privacy policy, service terms, retention practices, and contractual role apply to its independent processing. The precise data available to a provider depends on the deployment, access permissions, logging, and backup configuration.

The use of a hosting provider does not automatically transfer all privacy obligations of the Bot operator to that provider.

## 8. Discord and Other Third-Party Services

Gambo depends on Discord to provide its platform functionality. Discord processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

The Service may also use external APIs, databases, monitoring services, payment providers, community platforms, or other integrations if those features are enabled.

Third-party services may process information under their own policies and contractual arrangements. Users should review the relevant policies when interacting with those services.

The Service should disclose material additional data sharing where required by applicable law.

## 9. Disclosure of Information

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary and lawful:

- to hosting, infrastructure, database, or technical providers supporting the Service;
- to payment providers when paid features are offered;
- to providers whose integrations are enabled and needed for a requested feature;
- to competent authorities where disclosure is legally required;
- to protect the security of the Service, investigate suspected fraud, or enforce lawful rights;
- in connection with a lawful transfer or restructuring of the operation, where applicable legal requirements are met.

Information should not be sold to third parties as personal data unless such activity is lawfully permitted and all applicable disclosure and consent requirements are satisfied.

This Policy does not authorize disclosure for purposes incompatible with applicable data-protection law.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Because Discord, hosting, and other providers may operate internationally, information may be processed in countries other than the country where a user resides.

Where GDPR applies, transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area must comply with the relevant requirements for international transfers. Depending on the circumstances, this may involve an adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards, or another valid legal transfer mechanism.

The actual countries and safeguards depend on the providers and infrastructure used by the Service.

## 11. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, taking account of security, operational needs, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

For example:

- virtual economy records may be retained while needed to provide the associated features;
- technical logs may be retained for a limited period needed for security and troubleshooting;
- support messages may be retained while needed to resolve the issue and maintain an appropriate record;
- transaction records may be retained as required for accounting, legal, or dispute-related purposes.

Actual retention periods depend on the Bot's configuration, hosting provider, backups, and enabled integrations. This Policy does not promise a specific deletion deadline where one has not been established.

When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.

## 12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, accidental loss, alteration, disclosure, and destruction.

Possible measures include access controls, restricted credentials, software updates, secure configuration, backups, and limiting access to production systems.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. The effectiveness of these measures depends on the actual implementation and ongoing maintenance.

Users should not share secrets, passwords, payment credentials, or confidential information through Bot commands or public support channels.

## 13. Privacy Rights

Where GDPR or another applicable privacy law applies, users may have rights including:

- requesting access to their personal data;
- requesting correction of inaccurate data;
- requesting deletion of personal data in applicable circumstances;
- requesting restriction of processing;
- objecting to processing based on legitimate interests;
- receiving certain data in a portable format where the legal requirements are met;
- withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent;
- lodging a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. For example, deletion may not be required where data must be retained to comply with law or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

A request may require reasonable steps to verify that it relates to the correct person. Verification should be proportionate and should not require unnecessary sensitive information.
