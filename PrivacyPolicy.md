# 🔒 Gambo – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Gambo** ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described below.

---

### 1. Data We Collect 📊
We collect only the minimum data required for the bot to function correctly within the Discord environment:
* **Discord User ID:** A unique numerical ID used to identify your account and track your virtual balance.
* **Usernames:** Your Discord display name, used for leaderboards and in-game interactions.
* **Server (Guild) IDs:** Used to maintain separate economies and settings for each server.

### 2. How We Use Data ⚙️
The collected data is used exclusively for providing the core features of the Bot:
* Managing virtual currency balances and transaction history.
* Tracking daily streaks and rewards.
* Generating and displaying local and global leaderboards.
* Ensuring the randomized logic of gambling simulations (Blackjack, Slots, etc.).

### 3. Data Storage & Security 🛡️
* **Storage:** All data is stored in a private and secure SQLite database.
* **No Sharing:** We **do not** sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. 
* **Access:** Access to the database is strictly limited to the Bot developers for maintenance and support purposes.

### 4. Third-Party Services 🌐
Gambo is not affiliated with Discord Inc. The Bot does not use any external analytics or tracking scripts that monitor your behavior outside of direct interaction with the Bot's commands.

### 5. Limitation of Responsibility ⚠️
While we strive to protect your data, the developers are not responsible for data loss caused by Discord outages, server malfunctions, or unintended technical errors.
